# Thesis PDF Publication Status

## Current status

The complete corrected thesis PDF is intentionally withheld from this public folder while institutional similarity analysis may still be active.

This precaution reduces the risk that public GitHub indexing creates self-matches against the author's own dissertation before the institutional procedure is complete.

## What is preserved publicly

The repository preserves:

- thesis title and bibliographic information;
- scientific scope and interpretation limits;
- repository-to-thesis alignment;
- the SHA-256 checksum of the exact corrected PDF;
- canonical figures, tables, code, appendices, and reproducibility evidence, subject to their own publication review.

## Release condition

The full thesis PDF may be added only after both conditions are met:

1. The institution confirms that the required similarity review is complete or that public release will not affect the procedure.
2. The PDF selected for publication matches the canonical SHA-256 recorded in `05_version_control_and_hashes.md` and `canonical_thesis_reference.txt`.

## Canonical public filename

When release is authorized, use a stable filename such as:

`Tesis_Doctoral_Corregida_1053833697.pdf`

The original submitted file must remain archived privately under its original name and checksum.
