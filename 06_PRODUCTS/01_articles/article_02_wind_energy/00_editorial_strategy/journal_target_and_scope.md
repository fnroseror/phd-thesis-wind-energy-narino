# Article 02 — Target journal and editorial lock

## Primary target

**Wind Energy — Wiley**

Decision date: 2026-09-24.

## Why this journal fits the paper

The journal explicitly covers wind-resource assessment techniques involving prediction, modelling and atmospheric physics, and its stated keywords include wind power meteorology and forecasting. This maps directly onto the Article 02 scope: WPD prediction, multi-horizon forecasting, complex-terrain meteorology, benchmarking and uncertainty-aware evaluation.

The paper should be positioned as a wind-energy forecasting study with a physical-statistical interpretation, not as a generic machine-learning benchmark.

## Research Article requirements verified on 2026-09-24

- Research Article word limit: 8,000 words.
- Abstract: up to 250 words.
- Keywords: up to six.
- Main structure: Introduction; Materials and Methods; Results; Discussion; Conclusion.
- Data Availability Statement: required.
- ORCID: required.
- Supporting information: separate files.
- Free-format initial submission is supported.
- Supporting data and artefacts should be archived in an appropriate public repository.

## Publication-cost constraint

Wind Energy is fully Gold Open Access. The journal currently lists an APC of USD 3,730 / GBP 3,000 / EUR 3,470 after acceptance/publication, subject to institutional or funder coverage, discounts or waivers.

Editorial decision: retain Wind Energy as the primary scientific target, but verify funding or waiver feasibility before final submission.

## Target framing

Preferred framing: multi-horizon WPD forecasting in complex Andean terrain, with model benchmarking against persistence and explicit uncertainty/residual diagnostics.

Avoid framing the paper as a universal best-model claim, a generic AI comparison disconnected from wind physics, a turbine power forecast or a commercial wind-resource feasibility study.
