## Article identification

Physical–statistical characterization of wind power density in complex Andean terrain: A multi-station study in Nariño, Colombia

**Target journal:** Energy Reports  
**Article type:** Research Article  
**Current phase:** Manuscript construction and repository consolidation  

---

## Purpose

This file documents the planned declaration regarding the use of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies during the preparation of Article 01.

The purpose is to ensure editorial transparency while clearly distinguishing between:

1. scientific analysis performed by the author-controlled computational workflow;
2. manuscript organization and language support assisted by AI tools;
3. figures, tables and results generated directly from R scripts and research data.

---

## Draft declaration for manuscript

During the preparation of this work, the author used ChatGPT as an AI-assisted tool to support manuscript organization, editorial planning, language refinement and code-review discussion. All scientific analyses, data processing, statistical calculations, figures and tables were generated from the author-controlled research workflow in R. After using this tool, the author reviewed, edited and verified the content as needed and takes full responsibility for the content of the manuscript.

---

## Short version for submission system

During the preparation of this work, the author used ChatGPT for manuscript organization, editorial planning, language refinement and code-review discussion. All analyses, figures and tables were generated from the author-controlled research workflow in R. The author reviewed and verified the content and takes full responsibility for the manuscript.

## Important clarification

No generative AI tool was used to create or alter the scientific figures, plots, data visualizations, statistical results or numerical outputs reported in this article.

The figures were generated using R scripts from the analyzed meteorological data and derived outputs.

---

## Internal documentation of AI-assisted support

AI-assisted support was limited to:

```text
1. manuscript structure planning;
2. editorial organization;
3. repository organization;
4. checklist construction;
5. language refinement;
6. scientific wording support;
7. code-review discussion;
8. interpretation drafting under author supervision.
```

AI-assisted support was not used for:

```text
1. raw data generation;
2. fabrication of results;
3. alteration of figures;
4. generation of scientific plots as artificial images;
5. replacement of author judgment;
6. unsupervised manuscript writing;
7. unsupported citation creation;
8. manipulation of numerical outputs.
```

---

## Figure and artwork control statement

All scientific figures included in the manuscript and supplementary material must be generated from reproducible R scripts.

No submitted scientific figure, plot, chart or data visualization should be created or modified using generative AI image tools.

If a graphical abstract is prepared, it must be built from author-controlled schematic elements, original figures, vector shapes or non-AI editorial design components.
