## Cover letter — Draft version

Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled “Physical–statistical characterization of wind power density in complex Andean terrain: A multi-station study in Nariño, Colombia” for consideration as a Research Article in *Energy Reports*.

This study presents a physical–statistical characterization of wind power density in Nariño, Colombia, a complex Andean region with heterogeneous topographic and atmospheric conditions. The manuscript uses multi-station meteorological observations grouped into analytical zones to estimate wind power density from wind speed and air density, evaluate quality-controlled wind-energy behavior, compare Weibull and Rayleigh distributional models, and analyze temporal persistence and spectral structure through ACF/PACF and FFT-based methods.

The main contribution of the manuscript is a reproducible framework for characterizing wind-energy regimes in complex terrain using observational meteorological data. Rather than relying only on average wind speed, the study focuses on wind power density as a physically meaningful energy descriptor, incorporating air-density effects and the cubic dependence of wind speed. The results show marked zonal contrasts, with one analytical zone exhibiting a stronger and more intermittent wind-energy regime, while temporal and spectral analyses reveal distinct persistence and period-band structures across zones.

We believe this manuscript is suitable for *Energy Reports* because it addresses renewable energy assessment, wind-resource characterization, data-driven energy analysis, and methodological reproducibility in a region where localized wind-energy studies remain limited. The article contributes to the understanding of wind-energy potential in complex Andean terrain and provides a transparent computational workflow to support future regional energy studies.

We confirm that this manuscript has not been published previously and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to *Energy Reports*. The author(s) declare no competing interests unless otherwise specified in the final declaration files.

Thank you for considering our manuscript for publication.

Sincerely,
