# Cover letter draft

Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit the manuscript entitled “Physical–statistical characterization of wind power density in complex Andean terrain: A multi-station study in Nariño, Colombia” for consideration as a Research Article in Energy Reports.

This manuscript presents a physical–statistical and spectral characterization of wind power density in Nariño, Colombia, a complex Andean region with heterogeneous meteorological and topographic conditions. The study uses multi-station observational meteorological records from IDEAM for the 2017–2022 period, grouped into four analytical zones. Wind power density was estimated from wind speed and daily zone-level air density, and the analysis integrated quality control, descriptive statistics, Weibull and Rayleigh distribution fitting, temporal-dependence diagnostics and FFT-based spectral-band decomposition.

The main contribution of the manuscript is a reproducible framework for interpreting wind power density as a physical state variable rather than only as an average energy indicator. The results show that wind power density provides stronger physical separation among analytical zones than wind speed alone. In particular, Zone 1 showed intermittent energetic dominance associated with frequent low-speed states, a long high-speed tail and nonlinear cubic amplification in wind power density. The study also identified zone-dependent temporal memory and spectral-energy signatures, supporting a more complete characterization of wind-energy regimes in complex Andean terrain.

We believe this manuscript is suitable for Energy Reports because it contributes to renewable-energy assessment, regional wind-resource characterization and reproducible physical–statistical analysis under complex-terrain conditions. The study does not claim turbine-level feasibility, wind-farm investment viability or micrositing validation; rather, it provides regional physical–statistical evidence that can support future forecasting, uncertainty analysis, terrain-specific modeling and energy-planning studies.

All authors have approved the submitted version of the manuscript. The manuscript is original, has not been published previously and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere.
