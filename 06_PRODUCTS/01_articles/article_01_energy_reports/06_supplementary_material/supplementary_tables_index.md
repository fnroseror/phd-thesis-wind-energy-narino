## Supplementary Table S1

**File:** `Table_S01_station_key_for_Figure_1.csv`

**Title:** Station key for Figure 1.

**Description:**  
This table provides the station key used to identify the IDEAM meteorological stations displayed in Figure 1. Each station is represented in the map using a short label from S01 to S16. The table links each label with its IDEAM station code, station name, analytical zone and geographic coordinates.

**Use in manuscript:**  
This table supports the interpretation of Figure 1 and allows the reader to identify each meteorological station without overloading the main map with long station names.

---

## Supplementary Table S2

**File:** `Table_S02_zone_station_summary_for_Figure_1.csv`

**Title:** Analytical-zone station summary for Figure 1.

**Description:**  
This table summarizes the number of meteorological stations included in each analytical zone. The zones correspond to station groupings used for the physical–statistical characterization of wind power density in Nariño, Colombia.

**Use in manuscript:**  
This table supports the zone-based analytical design used throughout the article and complements Figure 1.
