# 10. Citation-density control

The manuscript should avoid excessive citation density.

Preferred citation logic:

```text
Introduction: moderate citation density
Methodology: strong methodological citation density
Results: low citation density
Discussion: selective interpretive citation density
Data availability: official source and reproducibility citations only
```

Avoid:

```text
overloaded paragraphs
citation stacking without explanation
references inserted only for volume
citations disconnected from claims
```

# 12. TDQ citation rule

A citation enters the manuscript only if it performs one of these functions:

```text
supports the physical formulation
justifies the methodological choice
contextualizes the region
supports interpretation of results
documents the data source
supports reproducibility
```

If a citation does not perform one of these functions, it is removed.
