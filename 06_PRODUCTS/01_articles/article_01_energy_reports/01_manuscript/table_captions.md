# Main tables

## Table 1

File:
`04_tables/main_text/Table_01_dataset_zones_temporal_coverage.xlsx`

Caption:
Table 1. Dataset, analytical zones and temporal coverage. The table summarizes the IDEAM meteorological stations, analytical zoning structure and temporal coverage used for the regional wind power density assessment in Nariño, Colombia. The analytical zones represent physical–spatial subdomains for comparing wind-speed variability, air-density-supported WPD and temporal behavior.

## Table 2

File:
`04_tables/main_text/Table_01_descriptive_WPD_summary_by_zone.xlsx`

Caption:
Table 2. Descriptive statistics of wind speed and wind power density by analytical zone. The table reports the number of records, central tendency, dispersion, selected percentiles and maximum values for wind speed and WPD after quality control. The results show that WPD provides stronger physical separation among zones than wind speed alone, especially in Zone 1, where intermittent high-speed states generate a disproportionate energetic contribution after the cubic transformation into WPD.

---

## Table 3

File:
`04_tables/main_text/Table_02_weibull_rayleigh_best_distribution_by_zone.xlsx`

Caption:
Table 3. Weibull and Rayleigh distribution parameters and goodness-of-fit metrics by analytical zone. The table summarizes the fitted distribution parameters and model-selection results used to characterize wind-speed probability structures. Weibull was selected as the best-performing distribution in all analytical zones, with Zone 1 showing a shape parameter below one, consistent with a strongly asymmetric and intermittent wind-speed regime.

## Table 4

File:
`04_tables/main_text/Table_03_temporal_dependence_summary_WPD_by_zone.xlsx`

Caption:
Table 4. Temporal and spectral summary of wind power density by analytical zone. The table integrates temporal-dependence indicators and FFT-based spectral-band energy to characterize WPD as a temporally structured physical state variable. The results show zone-dependent memory and spectral-energy signatures, including strong persistence and low-frequency dominance in Zone 1.

# Editorial control

The table captions are aligned with the final manuscript structure:

* Table 1 supports the study area, station grouping and analytical zoning.
* Table 2 supports the descriptive physical–statistical characterization of wind speed and WPD.
* Table 3 supports the Weibull/Rayleigh probabilistic characterization.
* Table 4 supports the temporal-memory and spectral-energy interpretation of daily WPD.

The tables should be interpreted as regional physical–statistical evidence, not as turbine-level feasibility, wind-farm micrositing or investment assessment outputs.
