## Article identification

**Provisional title:**  
Physical–statistical characterization of wind power density in complex Andean terrain: A multi-station study in Nariño, Colombia

**Target journal:** Energy Reports  
**Publisher:** Elsevier  
**Article type:** Research Article  
**Reference style:** Author–year / Elsevier Harvard style  

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the reference-selection protocol for Article 01.

The purpose is to ensure that all references included in the manuscript are:

```text
relevant
verified
traceable
journal-compliant
scientifically necessary
aligned with the article scope
```

This protocol prevents the inclusion of weak, duplicated, unsupported or non-verifiable references.

# 3. Inclusion criteria

A reference can be included if it supports at least one of the following article needs:

```text
wind power density formulation
wind-resource assessment
Weibull distribution in wind-energy studies
Rayleigh distribution in wind-energy studies
complex terrain wind assessment
air-density correction or physical formulation
temporal dependence in wind-speed or WPD series
ACF/PACF methodology
FFT or spectral analysis in meteorology or wind-energy studies
renewable-energy context in Colombia or Andean regions
official meteorological data source
research reproducibility
```

A reference should also meet at least one of these quality criteria:

```text
peer-reviewed article
recognized book or technical reference
official institutional document
methodological foundation
high relevance to the manuscript argument
verified DOI or official source
```

---

# 4. Exclusion criteria

A reference must be excluded if it is:

```text
not verifiable
not directly relevant to Article 01
duplicated by a stronger source
only loosely connected to the article
not cited in the manuscript
included only to increase reference count
focused on topics outside the article scope
unsupported by DOI, publisher record or official URL
```

References outside the article scope include:

```text
TDQ as philosophical theory
mental/decision theory
turbine-level design not used in this article
economic feasibility not analyzed here
grid integration not analyzed here
CFD studies unless used only for complex-terrain context
deep learning forecasting unless cited only as future work
```

# 6. Prioritization rule

Not all references have the same value.

Priority order:

```text
1. Directly relevant peer-reviewed wind-energy articles.
2. Foundational methodological articles or books.
3. Peer-reviewed complex-terrain wind studies.
4. Peer-reviewed time-series or spectral-analysis studies.
5. Colombia / Andes / regional energy studies.
6. Official institutional data sources.
7. Doctoral theses or technical reports only when uniquely useful.
```

Doctoral theses from the Excel may be used as background only if they provide unique conceptual value. For Energy Reports, peer-reviewed journal articles should dominate the final list.

---

# 7. DOI verification rule

For journal articles:

```text
DOI required when available
```

If DOI cannot be found:

```text
use official publisher URL
record reason why DOI is unavailable
avoid the reference if verification is weak
```

Never invent a DOI.

Never copy a DOI without checking that it corresponds to the exact title.

---

# 8. Manuscript citation rule

Every final reference must satisfy:

```text
cited in manuscript: yes
listed in references: yes
DOI or URL verified: yes
style corrected: yes
supports a specific claim: yes
```

No orphan references.

No uncited references.

No unsupported citations.

# 10. Reference extraction workflow

The workflow will follow this sequence:

```text
1. Review Revisión Sistemática.xlsx.
2. Identify candidate references by thematic block.
3. Select article-relevant candidates.
4. Verify each candidate externally.
5. Confirm DOI or official URL.
6. Classify each reference by manuscript use.
7. Insert in references_working_list.md.
8. Insert in-text citation in manuscript.
9. Build final author-year reference list.
10. Cross-check citations and references before submission.
```

# 13. TDQ reference rule

A reference enters the article only if it reduces uncertainty.

A reference is removed if it creates noise.

Final rule:

```text
If a reference does not support the problem, method, result, discussion or data traceability, it does not enter the article.
```
