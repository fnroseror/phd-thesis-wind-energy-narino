Figure X. Revised methodological framework for the physical–statistical characterization of wind power density in Nariño. The workflow separates data preparation, local energetic transformation, station-aware aggregation, sensitivity analysis, statistical inference, temporal and spectral diagnostics, and uncertainty quantification. The nonlinear WPD transformation is applied at station-hour scale before zonal aggregation because local cubic transformation and spatial averaging are not interchangeable operations. All zone-level results are interpreted conditionally on the observed station network.
