Figure X. Revised methodological framework for the physical–statistical characterization of wind power density in Nariño. Wind observations are first constructed at station-hour scale, quality controlled before the nonlinear energetic transformation, and converted to local station-hour WPD using daily zone-level air density. Local energetic states are then aggregated with equal weight among contributing stations at each zone-hour. Station-composition sensitivity, inter-zonal statistics, temporal and spectral diagnostics, and block-bootstrap uncertainty analysis are applied to delimit the interpretation of the resulting observational-network summaries.
